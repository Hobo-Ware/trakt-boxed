Anatomy of a Fall 2023
★★★★½ Liked DIRECTED BY JUSTINE TRIET · 152 MIN

A courtroom drama where the verdict is the least interesting thing in the room. Justine Triet films a marriage like a crime scene: every object is evidence, every sentence gets played back, and nobody in the audience is allowed to be sure of anything.

Sandra Hüller never once asks you to like Sandra. She switches languages when she wants distance, laughs at the wrong moments, and answers questions with the precision of someone who has been misquoted before. It is the best performance I have seen this decade, and it only works because the film refuses to reward her for it.

The centerpiece is the recorded argument. Triet starts it as audio in the courtroom and slides into the kitchen without warning, so you are suddenly in the fight with them. When it cuts back to the tape, it feels like being caught eavesdropping.

Third time watching and it gets harder, not easier. Snoop the dog remains the only honest witness. Half a star off for the music, which I still cannot hear without flinching.
